about=Coverage Prediction is a QGIS plugin that mirrors the propagationpredict.onrender.com workflow. It samples a digital elevation model along the antenna bearing, evaluates the main, upper and lower beam profiles, estimates received signal level along the radial, and renders the coverage footprint as a graduated layer on the QGIS map canvas. The result can be exported to KMZ for use in Google Earth.
